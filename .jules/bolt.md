## 2024-05-20 - Frustum Culling and Controls Disposal
**Learning:** When enabling `frustumCulled={true}` in React Three Fiber, an `InstancedMesh` requires the bounding sphere to be manually computed on the instance itself (e.g., `meshRef.current.computeBoundingSphere()`), NOT its geometry. For `<points>` or `<line>` with a manually constructed `<bufferGeometry>`, the bounding sphere must be computed directly on the geometry. Incorrect bounding sphere management leads to objects being incorrectly culled. Additionally, explicitly calling `controls.dispose()` on Drei controls like `OrbitControls` inside cleanup functions interferes with Drei's internal lifecycle management and should be avoided.
**Action:** Always compute bounding spheres correctly based on the object type when enabling frustum culling, and rely on Drei to manage the lifecycle of its own controls.
