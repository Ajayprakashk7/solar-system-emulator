## 2024-05-18 - InstancedMesh Culling in Three.js
**Learning:** In Three.js, setting `frustumCulled={false}` on an `InstancedMesh` forces the GPU to render all instances even when off-screen, degrading performance. However, you cannot fix this simply by padding the underlying `geometry.boundingSphere` because an `InstancedMesh` manages its own bounding sphere to encapsulate the spatial spread of all its instances. Modifying the geometry's sphere instead leads to incorrect culling (premature culling/popping) when the camera moves.
**Action:** When enabling frustum culling on an `InstancedMesh`, always compute the bounding sphere directly on the mesh instance using `meshRef.current.computeBoundingSphere()` after updating the instance matrices. For simple `Points` meshes, computing/padding the `geometry.boundingSphere` is sufficient.
