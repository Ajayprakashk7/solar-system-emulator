## 2026-09-24 - Frustum Culling on Instanced Mesh & Points
**Learning:** `InstancedMesh` and `Points` components using `frustumCulled={false}` defeat a crucial optimization mechanism in R3F/Three.js, especially for objects like AsteroidBelts and CosmicDust that span large portions of the scene. When `frustumCulled={true}` is set, Three.js might cull the objects incorrectly on mount if the bounding sphere is not properly initialized.
**Action:** Always enable `frustumCulled={true}` for large particle systems and instanced meshes. To prevent incorrect culling on initial mount (due to uninitialized matrices yielding a zero-radius sphere), manually construct and assign a fixed bounding sphere encompassing the maximum expected bounds. For `InstancedMesh`, assign it to `meshRef.current.boundingSphere`. For `Points`, assign it to `meshRef.current.geometry.boundingSphere`.
